## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Maintain a responsive engineering-product design system
The frontend SHALL use semantic tokens for canvas, panels, type, spacing, borders, radii, controls, code, status, and motion. It SHALL be usable at 1366×768, 1440×900, and 1920×1080 desktop viewports without a headline or control collision.

#### Scenario: User views the product at supported desktop sizes
- **WHEN** the viewport is 1366×768, 1440×900, or 1920×1080
- **THEN** the first viewport SHALL retain readable hierarchy, visible primary action, and an unclipped workspace preview
